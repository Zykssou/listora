Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who we are

Listora is an AI-powered Amazon listing generator. We are an independent service. Contact: contact@listora.io

2. What data we collect

  • Your email address — used only to count your free listings and prevent abuse
  • The product information you enter (product name, features, keywords) — sent to Google's Gemini AI to generate your listing
  • The generated listing — saved to track usage statistics

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No passwords
  • No payment information
  • No browsing history or cookies (except language preference stored locally in your browser)
  • No personal information beyond your email

4. How we use your data

  • To count how many free listings you have used
  • To display anonymous usage statistics (total users, total listings)
  • We never sell, share, or rent your data to third parties

5. Third-party services

Your listing prompts are processed by Google Gemini AI. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

The site is hosted on Render.com. Render's privacy policy: render.com/privacy

6. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to data processing

To exercise these rights, email us at contact@listora.io

7. Data retention

We retain your email and usage data for as long as the service operates. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related questions: contact@listora.io
